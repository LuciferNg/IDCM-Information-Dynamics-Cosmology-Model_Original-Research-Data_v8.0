# P14: Pseudo-Goldstone Boson from Sync SSB

## Problem
The sync transition's SSB produces a pseudo-Goldstone boson. IDCM must predict its mass and decay modes.

## IDCM Result
- M_PG = ε·M_Z = 0.1545 × 91.188 = **14.09 GeV**
- Dominant decay: PG → bb̄ (67%), ττ̄ (9%), cc̄ (3%)
- Production: Z → γ + PG (BR ≈ 2.5×10⁻⁷)
